Support

Bank Tycoon for iPhone

Contact

Email hellolpworks@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

I bought Premium but it's locked

Open Premium on the main menu and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my saved games?

On your iPhone only. The game saves automatically every month, and you can keep extra saves in the save slots (3 free, 10 with Premium). Deleting the app deletes your saves, unless they're in a device backup you restore.

Is this real banking advice?

No. Bank Tycoon is a fictional simulation. All banks, people and events in it are made up, and nothing in the game is financial advice.
