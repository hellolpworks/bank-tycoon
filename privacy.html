Privacy policy

Bank Tycoon for iPhone · Last updated October 11, 2026

The short version

Bank Tycoon collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the game never sends your information to us or anyone else.

Your games stay on your device

Saved games and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are included in your device backups (such as iCloud Backup) if you have those turned on, which Apple manages. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The Premium unlock is sold by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The game asks Apple on your device whether Premium is unlocked. Apple's privacy policy covers the purchase.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and performance data. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the game and on this page with a new date.
